Stop 2: Miami

In Miami, my team won back-to-back state championships while playing a national schedule. Every week meant a tough opponent, and the level of competition was some of the highest in the country.

Along the way I played against some of the best players in basketball, including the Boozer twins, AJ Dybantsa, Tyran Stokes, Kiyan Anthony, and VJ Edgecombe. Competing against players like that every night made me sharper and showed me what it takes to play at the highest level.

My senior year was the peak. I played over 25 minutes in big games and stepped up with free throws to ice the state championship game, a moment that capped off my time in Miami.
