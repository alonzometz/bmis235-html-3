Stop 3: Spokane

In Spokane, I walked on to the Gonzaga basketball team. I redshirted my freshman year, which gave me time to learn the system, get stronger, and grow as a player and a teammate.

During that time I learned a lot from veterans like Graham Ike, Braeden Smith, and Jalen Warley, and I played on the scout team, helping prepare the starters for each opponent. Every practice was a chance to compete against high-level players and improve.

This year I played my first college minutes and grabbed 2 rebounds. I also lost a tooth in the game, and the community rallied around me with a wave of support. It was a memorable first step in my Gonzaga career, and the journey is still going.
