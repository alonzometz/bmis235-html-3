Stop 1: Portland

Portland is where my basketball journey began. I played AAU ball for the Eastside Sonics and suited up for my school team at All Saints School. Those early years built the foundation for everything that came after, teaching me how to compete, work with teammates, and love the game.

I later moved on to Jesuit High School, where I played through my sophomore year. Playing at a bigger program pushed me to get better and prepared me for the stops that were still ahead.

Two highlights stand out from my time in Portland. The first was hitting a game-winner against Sunset High School, a moment I still remember clearly. The second was having my grandpa around to teach me how to shoot, which shaped my game and made the sport even more meaningful to me.
